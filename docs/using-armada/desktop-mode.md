From the Steam power menu, choose **Switch to Desktop** for a full KDE Plasma
desktop. The **Bazaar** app store and the **Armada Installer**
([Install to internal storage](../getting-started/install-to-internal-storage.md)) live here. Use the
**Return to Gaming Mode** shortcut on the desktop to switch back.

Additionally, Armada supports an alternative KDE Plasma Mobile for a more touch-
friendly environment. This may be toggled in the **Settings** tab of
**[Armada Control](./armada-control.md)**.
